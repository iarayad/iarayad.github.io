Hi!👋 I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist working on mesoscopic superconductivity, topology, and quantum transport.

📍 Currently I am a postdoc at DIPC in San Sebastián, Spain, working with Adolfo Grushin.

💻 I completed my PhD in the Quantum Tinkerer group at Delft University of Technology, The Netherlands, with Anton Akhmerov and Michael Wimmer, and defended in February 2026.

I particularly enjoy working on problems that require combining analytics and numerics. One of my favorite projects so far was developing Pymablock, an open-source Python package to do quantum mechanical perturbation theory efficiently, numerically and symbolically.

PhD. Condensed matter physics, Delft University of Technology · 2021 → 2026

I got a Casimir NanoFront grant to do my PhD in QuTech and defended cum laude on February 27, 2026. During this time I had the opportunity to improve my coding skills, work collaboratively, and learn about open science practices ✨.

  • Worked on Andreev physics, topological invariants, and mesoscopic physics (see Research).
  • Developed an open-source Python package, Pymablock.
  • Worked as a teaching assistant of the solid state physics course.

Research visit at an experimental lab, Cornell University · 2024

I visited and collaborated with Valla Fatemi’s group in Ithaca for a month.

  • Worked on a read-out scheme for Andreev spin qubits using inductive dispersive sensing.
  • Optimized time-dependent pulses for qubit control using the Krotov Python package.

Postdoctoral researcher, Donostia International Physics Center · 2025 → present

I joined Adolfo Grushin’s group in San Sebastián to work on DMRG and tensor networks applied to topological disordered systems.
